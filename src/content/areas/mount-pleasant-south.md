I'll be straight about this one. Mount Pleasant south of the Connector is not a small-multifamily market. Only a handful of small buildings changed hands in the last year, and that is not enough to put a typical price on one. If you want a duplex or a fourplex here, plan to wait, and plan to move fast when one shows up.

The older part of town has a few of them. You will find duplexes and cottages near the Old Village, and a few small apartment buildings along Coleman Boulevard. Past that, the stock is mostly single-family homes and townhomes, some old and some new. When a small building does list, it is priced at a premium, because there are so few to compare it to.

That shapes how I would think about a deal. A house here costs a lot, and rents are high too. The question is whether the rent carries the price once you add the loan, the tax, and insurance. Run it with a real lease comp from the same side of town. Do not borrow a number from a cheaper area.

One more thing I would check first. The Town sets its own rules, and they can limit what you do with a lot or a building. I'd rather you learn that before you make an offer than after. If you have an address in mind, call or text me and we will look at it together.
