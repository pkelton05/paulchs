Stratton Capers is the older and cheaper side of this area. Its typical home dates to the mid-1960s, against the late 1980s in Woodington, and the median price is well below Woodington's over the same two-year measure. Prices spread widely from one sale to the next, so the condition of the house matters more here than the street name. Nearly all sales are detached homes, and most are free of an HOA, which sets it apart from the other three neighborhoods in this group.
