Shady Oaks is attached housing, and nearly all of it is new: the sales were all townhome-style homes, and the typical one was built around 2020. Prices cluster tightly, so the middle half of sales sits in a narrow band. That makes it easy to tell a fair price from a stretch. Because there are few sales a year, the price shown is a two-year median, not a one-year read.

Fewer homes sold in the latest twelve months than in the year before, and the typical one took a while to sell. That gives a buyer some room to talk. On rent, the three-bedroom number is an area-wide one rather than a Shady Oaks lease figure, because only two leases here were for three bedrooms. The four-bedroom-and-up rent does come from leases here, but from a small handful, so use it as a loose guide.
