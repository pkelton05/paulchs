Carolina Cove is the oldest townhome neighborhood in this area's data, with a typical build date in the early 1970s. Every sale was an attached unit, and almost all are in an HOA. Prices sit in the same low range as Ashleytowne Village, well below the detached-home neighborhoods like Shadowmoss and Springfield.

This is also the quickest seller of the neighborhoods here with a median time on market. Sales in the latest twelve months were more than double the prior year. Carolina Cove has the most of its own lease data of this group: 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom rents come from neighborhood leases, though the samples are small. The 3-bedroom rent comes in well under the area figure, so do not borrow the area number for a deal here. The 1-bedroom and 4-bedroom rents are area-level.
