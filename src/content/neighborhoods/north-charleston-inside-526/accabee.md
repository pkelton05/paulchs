Accabee is a thin market, so read these numbers as a rough guide. Sales here are all detached houses of about the same era, and they are slow to come: only a few sold in two years, most of them more than a year ago, and they took months to find a buyer. The price range is wide, with a few sales well above the rest. There is only one lease under this name, so every rent on this page is an area figure for North Charleston inside I-526. For a house here, I would build the rent estimate from comps in Waylyn and Dorchester Terrace.
