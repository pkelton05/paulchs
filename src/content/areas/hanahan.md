Hanahan is a small city wedged between North Charleston and Goose Creek. It is easy to drive through and miss. For an investor, the draw is location. The Naval Weapons Station, the port, and I-26 are all close, and that is where the rent demand comes from.

You might expect a mix of single-family homes, duplexes, and townhomes here. The numbers say it is mostly houses. Just one small multifamily building sold in the past year, so a search for one could take a long time. Plan on buying a single-family home or a townhome and running it as a rental.

Rent data here is thinner than in the bigger areas next door. I have a decent number of three-bedroom leases and fewer two-bedroom ones. For one-bedroom and larger homes, I don't have enough leases to quote a number I'd stand behind. If I'm running a deal in Hanahan, I look at nearby North Charleston leases too, and I say which is which.
