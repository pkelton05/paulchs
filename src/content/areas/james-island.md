James Island is close to downtown and close to the beach, and that shows up in how fast things sell. Inventory is tight. When a duplex or fourplex is listed, it does not sit long, so you want your lender and inspector lined up before you go see it.

The buildings are a plain mix. Ranch houses from the middle of the last century. Duplexes, some built that way and some converted. A few small apartment buildings, mostly along Folly Road and Camp Road. A two- to four-unit building here sells for a bit more than a single-family house, which is the opposite of a few other parts of the county. That tells me investors are paying for the extra doors.

The rules here are the thing I would learn first. Parts of the island sit in the Town of James Island, parts in the City of Charleston, and parts in the county. Each one has its own zoning and its own approach to rentals. Two buildings a block apart can answer to different offices. Before you fall for a building, find out which one you are in.

Then go to the marsh side of the map and look at the flood zone. After that, run the rent against recent lease comps for the same bedroom count. I like this area for long-term rentals with a short drive to the peninsula. Send me an address and I will go through it with you.
