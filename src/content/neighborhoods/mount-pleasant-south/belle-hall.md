Belle Hall is a single-family market. Every sale in the record was a detached house, and the typical house went up in the late 1990s. No small multifamily building has sold here in the past two years, so this is a place to buy one house and rent it, not to look for a duplex. Sale volume held level against the year before, detached-home prices slipped a little, and a typical listing found a buyer faster than one in Snee Farm.

Study the price spread. The upper end of the range is more than double the lower end, so a median alone says little about what one house will cost. Only the three-bedroom rent comes from leases in Belle Hall itself, and it lands below the Snee Farm three-bedroom. The one-, two- and four-bedroom rents are Mount Pleasant south area figures, and the four-bedroom figure has only one Belle Hall lease behind it. Look up a comp on the exact street before leaning on any of them.
