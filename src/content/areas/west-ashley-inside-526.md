If the peninsula is out of reach, West Ashley inside the interstate is where I send people to look next. It sits just across the river from downtown. The housing is mostly brick ranches from the middle of the last century, with duplexes and small apartment buildings mixed in along the big roads.

Here is the honest part. Not many small multifamily buildings trade in this area. In a year you can count them on your hands. That means the numbers above are a rough guide, not a firm price list. One or two odd sales can move the median a lot. I would lean on the price per door and then look at the actual comps for the block, not the area as a whole.

Single-family houses cost more than a small multifamily building here, on a sticker basis. That flips the usual pattern and it is worth sitting with. A duplex can buy you two doors for less than the house next to it. But it also means a lot of your competition is people shopping for houses to rent out. If you are patient, you can wait for the building you actually want instead of settling for what is listed this week.

Do not skip the flood map and the sewer line. They matter more here than a fresh coat of paint does. Check them on the address, not on the neighborhood, and price anything you find into your offer.
