Oakfield is a neighborhood of detached houses, all of them built in the last several years. Sales here ran faster than in Kiawah River, and the spread between the cheaper and pricier homes is fairly tight, with the pricier half reaching a bit further above the median than the cheaper half sits below it. Detached-home prices rose a little over the past year. You will not find duplexes or fourplexes in the sales record.

The leases tell a narrower story than the sales. Every lease on record under this name is for a large house, so the four-bedroom-and-up rent comes from Oakfield itself. The three-bedroom and two-bedroom figures are Johns Island numbers, because Oakfield had only a couple of three-bedroom leases and none at the smaller size. Treat the larger-home rent as a rough guide, since it rests on a short list of leases, and ask me for comps before you underwrite.
