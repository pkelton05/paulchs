Small buildings trade often enough in Dorchester Terrace to show a pattern. Duplexes through fourplexes change hands several times a year, and the typical building sells for less than the typical house. That is unusual, and it is why the price per door is low. The middle half of building sales is narrow at the bottom and stretches out at the top, so the cheap end looks tightly bunched and the upper end is where condition and unit count start to matter.

Houses are the bulk of what sells, and nearly all are older detached homes. Prices rose over the past year even though fewer homes sold than the year before, and a typical house is under contract in about a month. Every rent shown is a neighborhood figure, with the largest units backed by the fewest leases. One-bedroom and two-bedroom rents sit close together, so an extra bedroom does not add much here. Check comps for the exact unit before you trust an average.
